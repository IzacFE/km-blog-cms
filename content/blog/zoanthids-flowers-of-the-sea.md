Zoanthids, or what people like to call "Zoa", are a very popular soft coral that's very easy to take care of, with such vibrant colors that can make any reef tank look spectacular.

## What are Zoanthids?

Zoanthids are colonial cnidarians that belong to the order Zoantharia. They form colonies of polyps that are connected by a mat of tissue called coenenchyme. Each polyp has a mouth surrounded by tentacles, and they come in a wide variety of colors and patterns.

## Why They're Popular

- **Easy to care for**: They don't require intense lighting or high water flow
- **Fast growing**: They can quickly spread and cover rockwork
- **Colorful**: Available in almost every color imaginable
- **Affordable**: Most zoanthids are reasonably priced

## Care Requirements

### Lighting
- Low to moderate lighting (PAR 50-150)
- Can adapt to various lighting conditions
- Avoid sudden changes in light intensity

### Water Flow
- Low to moderate water flow
- Too much flow can cause polyps to not open fully
- Gentle flow helps bring food to the polyps

### Water Parameters
- Temperature: 72-78°F (22-26°C)
- pH: 8.1-8.4
- Alkalinity: 8-12 dKH
- Calcium: 400-450 ppm
- Magnesium: 1250-1350 ppm

## Feeding

While zoanthids get most of their nutrition from their zooxanthellae (symbiotic algae), they can benefit from:
- Occasional feeding with phytoplankton
- Direct feeding with small meaty foods (brine shrimp, mysis)
- Target feeding can help with growth and coloration

## Safety Note

Some zoanthids contain palytoxin, a potent neurotoxin. Always:
- Wear gloves when handling
- Avoid touching your face or eyes
- Work in a well-ventilated area
- Never boil or burn zoanthids

## Placement

Place zoanthids on rockwork or substrate where they can:
- Receive appropriate lighting
- Get gentle water flow
- Have room to grow and spread
- Not be shaded by larger corals

## Conclusion

Zoanthids are excellent corals for beginners and experienced reef keepers alike. Their vibrant colors, ease of care, and fast growth make them a perfect addition to any reef tank.
