Bounce mushroom corals are among the most eye-catching and sought-after corals in the reef aquarium hobby. What sets them apart is their distinctive bubble-like texture that seems to "bounce" light in all directions.

## What Makes Bounce Mushrooms Special

Bounce mushrooms (Discosoma spp.) are prized for their:

- **Unique texture**: Bubble-like vesicles on the oral disc
- **Vibrant colors**: Intense blues, greens, reds, and purples
- **Water bubbles**: Small water-filled bubbles that reflect light
- **Movement**: Gentle swaying in water flow

## Appearance

The defining characteristic of bounce mushrooms is the presence of:

- Water-filled vesicles on the oral disc
- Smooth, bubble-like texture
- Metallic or iridescent sheen
- Often multiple colors in one specimen

## Care Requirements

### Lighting

- Low to moderate lighting
- PAR 50-100 is ideal
- Can bleach under intense lighting
- Acclimate slowly to new lighting

### Water Flow

- Low to moderate flow
- Too much flow can damage the bubbles
- Gentle flow helps with nutrient exchange

### Water Parameters

- Temperature: 72-78°F (22-26°C)
- pH: 8.1-8.4
- Alkalinity: 8-12 dKH
- Stable parameters are crucial

## Placement and Growth

- Place on rockwork or substrate
- Provide space for expansion
- Can move slowly over time
- May split and form new colonies

## Feeding

While they have zooxanthellae, bounce mushrooms benefit from:

- Direct feeding with meaty foods
- Mysis shrimp, brine shrimp
- Occasional phytoplankton
- Target feeding weekly

## Common Issues

### Losing Bubbles

- Can happen due to stress
- Check water parameters
- Ensure proper lighting
- Reduce flow if needed

### Bleaching

- Usually from too much light
- Move to lower light area
- Check for temperature spikes
- Ensure stable water quality

## Propagation

Bounce mushrooms can be propagated by:

- Cutting through the stalk with a sharp blade
- Using a rubber band method
- Detaching from the rock and cutting
- Allow cuttings to heal before placing

## Conclusion

Bounce mushrooms are stunning additions to any reef tank. Their unique appearance and relatively easy care requirements make them popular among reef enthusiasts. With proper care, they can thrive and become the centerpiece of your aquarium.
