Jawbreaker mushrooms are one of the most recognizable and prized corals in the reef aquarium hobby. They belong to the Discosoma genus and are known for their incredible color patterns that resemble the popular jawbreaker candy.

## What is a Jawbreaker Mushroom?

Jawbreaker mushrooms are a morph of Discosoma mushrooms characterized by:

- **Concentric color rings**: Multiple colored circles radiating from the center
- **High contrast**: Bold, distinct color boundaries
- **Metallic sheen**: Often have an iridescent quality
- **Multiple colors**: Can include red, blue, green, orange, and purple

## Color Patterns

The classic jawbreaker pattern features:

- Central color (often red or orange)
- Surrounding rings of different colors
- Sharp transitions between colors
- Symmetrical or asymmetrical patterns

## Care Requirements

### Lighting

- Low to moderate lighting preferred
- PAR 50-100 is ideal
- Too much light can fade colors
- Gradual acclimation is essential

### Water Flow

- Low to moderate flow
- Gentle flow is best
- Avoid direct, strong flow
- Flow helps with nutrient exchange

### Water Parameters

- Temperature: 72-78°F (22-26°C)
- pH: 8.1-8.4
- Alkalinity: 8-12 dKH
- Stable parameters are critical for color retention

## Placement

- Place on rockwork or substrate
- Provide adequate space for growth
- Avoid shading by taller corals
- Can be placed lower in the tank

## Feeding

Jawbreaker mushrooms benefit from:

- Direct feeding with small meaty foods
- Mysis shrimp, brine shrimp
- Occasional phytoplankton
- Target feeding 1-2 times per week

## Color Maintenance

To maintain vibrant colors:

- Stable water parameters
- Appropriate lighting
- Regular feeding
- Avoid parameter swings
- Monitor for signs of stress

## Common Issues

### Color Fading

- Often due to lighting changes
- Check water quality
- Ensure proper nutrition
- Reduce light intensity if needed

### Retraction

- Can happen with parameter changes
- Check for pests
- Verify water flow
- Allow time to recover

## Propagation

Propagate jawbreaker mushrooms by:

- Cutting through the stalk
- Using the rubber band method
- Detaching and slicing
- Allow healing before placement

## Value and Rarity

Jawbreaker mushrooms are:

- Highly sought after
- More expensive than standard mushrooms
- Considered collector's items
- Prices vary based on pattern quality

## Conclusion

Jawbreaker Discosoma mushrooms are stunning corals that can add incredible visual interest to any reef tank. Their unique color patterns and relatively easy care make them prized additions for serious reef keepers. With proper care, they can maintain their vibrant colors and become a showpiece in your aquarium.
